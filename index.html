DataLab workspace capsule profile

A DataLab workspace capsule (.dlcapsule) is a ZIP archive holding a DataLab HDF5 workspace and an RO-Crate manifest describing the processing provenance of its data.

  • Profile, version 0.1 (experimental): https://datalab-platform.com/capsule/0.1
  • Terms: https://datalab-platform.com/capsule/terms#
  • Reference implementation: DataLab-Capsule
